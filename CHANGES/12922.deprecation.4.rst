Renamed ``ThreadedResolver.close()`` and ``AsyncResolver.close()`` to ``aclose()``. ``close()`` still works but now emits a :class:`DeprecationWarning` and will be removed in aiohttp 5.0. ``AbstractResolver.close()`` stays the required abstract method for custom resolver implementations, so existing third-party resolvers keep working with no changes on their end. A new ``AbstractResolver.aclose()`` is available on every resolver, built-in or custom, and forwards to ``close()`` by default. Second slice of the rename tracked in :issue:`12922`, following the connector classes -- by :user:`ishan-1010`.
