Renamed ``ClientWebSocketResponse.close()`` and ``WebSocketResponse.close()`` to ``aclose()``. ``close()`` still works but now emits a :class:`DeprecationWarning` and will be removed in aiohttp 5.0. Both classes are concrete, user-facing objects (returned by ``ClientSession.ws_connect()`` and constructed directly in server handlers), not subclassing extension points, so both got the full rename treatment rather than the backward-compat forwarding used for ``AbstractResolver`` and ``Payload``. Updated the documented graceful-shutdown examples in ``docs/web_advanced.rst`` and ``docs/faq.rst`` to use ``aclose()``. Fourth slice of the rename tracked in :issue:`12922`, following the connector, resolver, and payload classes -- by :user:`ishan-1010`.
