Renamed ``BaseConnector.close()`` (and its ``TCPConnector``, ``UnixConnector``, ``NamedPipeConnector`` subclasses) to ``aclose()``, matching the convention :func:`contextlib.aclosing` expects and that other async libraries (trio, httpx) already use. ``close()`` still works but now emits a :class:`DeprecationWarning` and will be removed in aiohttp 5.0. This is the first of several classes covered by the broader ``close()`` -> ``aclose()`` rename tracked in :issue:`12922`; client session, websocket, payload, and resolver classes are still ``close()``-only for now -- by :user:`ishan-1010`.
