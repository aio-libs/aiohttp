Fixed :meth:`MultipartWriter.as_bytes() <aiohttp.MultipartWriter.as_bytes>`
returning raw part content for parts that declare ``Content-Encoding`` or
``Content-Transfer-Encoding``, while ``write()`` sends the compressed/encoded
content on the wire. Quoted-printable parts are now encoded line by line, so
the wire bytes no longer depend on how the payload was chunked (previously a
line ending, long line, or trailing whitespace split across payload chunks was
encoded differently, and a CRLF split across chunks could decode incorrectly).
The mismatch also produced an incorrect entity hash for digest authentication
with ``qop=auth-int`` -- by :user:`2sumtech`.
