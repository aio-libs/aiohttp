Renamed ``ClientSession.close()`` to ``aclose()``. ``close()`` still works but now emits a :class:`DeprecationWarning` and will be removed in aiohttp 5.0. ``ClientSession`` is ``@final``, nobody subclasses it, so this got the straightforward rename plus a deprecated shim rather than the backward-compat forwarding used for ``AbstractResolver`` and ``Payload``. Updated the persistent-session and quickstart examples in ``docs/client_advanced.rst`` and ``docs/client_quickstart.rst`` to use ``aclose()``. Final slice of the rename tracked in :issue:`12922`, following the connector, resolver, payload, and websocket classes -- by :user:`ishan-1010`.
