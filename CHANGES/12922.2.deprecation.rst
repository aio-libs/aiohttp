Renamed ``IOBasePayload.close()`` and ``BytesIOPayload.close()`` to ``aclose()``. ``close()`` still works but now emits a :class:`DeprecationWarning` and will be removed in aiohttp 5.0. The base ``Payload.close()`` is unchanged and not deprecated, since ``Payload`` is a public extension point and forcing every custom payload's ``close()`` override to move immediately would be a much harder break than a warning. A new ``Payload.aclose()`` is available on every payload, built-in or custom, and forwards to ``close()`` by default. Third slice of the rename tracked in :issue:`12922`, following the connector and resolver classes -- by :user:`ishan-1010`.
