Reject WebSocket frames that the protocol forbids instead of writing them and
letting the peer tear the connection down: ``ping()``/``pong()`` payloads longer
than 125 bytes, close status codes that may not appear on the wire (including
1006), and close reasons that are not valid UTF-8 now raise ``ValueError`` before
anything is sent, matching the checks the reader already applies.
