Fixed :py:class:`~aiohttp.web.StreamResponse` sending body bytes in responses
to ``HEAD`` requests and in ``204`` and ``304`` responses: data passed to
:py:meth:`~aiohttp.web.StreamResponse.write` and the stream flushed by
:py:meth:`~aiohttp.web.StreamResponse.enable_compression` (for example on a
``304`` from :py:class:`~aiohttp.web.FileResponse`) followed the headers with
no framing and was read as the start of the next response on a keep-alive
connection -- by :user:`insaf021`.
