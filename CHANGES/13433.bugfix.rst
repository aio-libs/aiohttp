Fixed dynamic resources and static prefixes whose fixed part needs
percent-encoding (a space or non-ASCII characters, for example) returning
``404``. They are now matched against the unquoted request path, the way plain
resources already were, so the URLs built by ``url_for()`` route back to their
resource. As a consequence the route metadata reports those fixed parts
without percent-encoding as well: ``canonical`` on dynamic and prefix-based
resources, and the ``prefix`` entry of ``get_info()`` on static and
sub-application resources -- by :user:`loonister1`.
